# Awards to check

Documentary Club shows awards on film cards, film pages and the Awards tab (/awards). Every award has to be real and sourced, so this is a check of the whole catalogue: 576 films, 165 of which have at least one award listed now.

## The job

1. **Work award by award, not film by film.** For each award below, go through its official list of winners (and Oscar nominees) for documentary categories, and pick out any film in our catalogue.
2. **Report what's missing.** Any award a catalogue film should have that isn't in its "Awards now" column.
3. **Report what's wrong.** Any award listed that you can't confirm, or that has the wrong year or category.

**Start with Tribeca, IDA and Emmy.** Our usual source (Wikidata) has no data for them, so they're almost certainly incomplete: we have only 2 Tribeca, 5 IDA and 12 Emmy wins at the moment.

## Awards we show (nothing else)

Use these labels exactly, with the **year of the ceremony or festival** at the end.

| Award | Label to use | What counts |
|---|---|---|
| Academy Awards | `Oscar Winner 2024` / `Oscar Nominee 2024` | Best Documentary Feature, win or nomination |
| Academy Awards, other categories | `Oscar Nominee – Best Film Editing 1995` | A documentary nominated or winning outside the documentary category: say which |
| BAFTA | `BAFTA Winner 2024` | Wins only, documentary category |
| Primetime Emmy | `Emmy Winner 2023` | Wins only. Primetime Emmys, not News & Documentary Emmys |
| Sundance | `Sundance Grand Jury Prize 2023`, `Sundance Audience Award 2023`, `Sundance Directing Award 2024` | Documentary categories (US or World Cinema) |
| Tribeca | `Tribeca Best Documentary Feature 2007` | Best Documentary Feature (jury) |
| IDA Documentary Awards | `IDA Best Feature 2016` / `IDA Best Episodic Series 2017` | Wins only |
| Cannes | `Cannes Palme d'Or 2004` / `Cannes L'Œil d'or 2023` | Wins only |

Leave out everything else: other festivals, critics' awards, "official selection", shortlists and nominations other than the Oscars.

## Reply format

One line per change, with a link to the official source (the award body's own site, or Wikipedia's winners page if that's all there is):

`site id | add / remove / fix | label | source URL`

For example: `hoop-dreams | add | Sundance Audience Award 1994 | https://...`

## Notes on the "Awards now" column

- Labels marked "(no year; from IMDb summary)" come from IMDb's one-line award summary, which only gives a count, never the category or year. Where you can confirm one, send the full label with its year and it will replace the summary version.
- "Series" rows are TV series, which can win Emmys and IDA's series award.
- Ignore any film not in this table.
